ClawExperts is committed to protecting your privacy.

This Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data.

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We may collect:

  • Account information
  • Email address
  • Registration information
  • Usage data
  • Actions performed on the platform
  • Agent configurations
  • System logs
  • User-provided data
  • Content, inputs, and data submitted to agents
2. HOW WE USE DATA

We use data to:

  • Operate and maintain the service
  • Improve system performance
  • Support users

We do not use your data to train AI models.

3. ISOLATED ENVIRONMENTS

Each agent runs in an isolated environment.

This means:

  • Data is not shared between users
  • Agents cannot access one another
4. DATA SHARING

We do not sell or share your personal data.

Data may only be shared:

  • When required by law
  • With infrastructure providers necessary to operate the service, such as cloud service providers
5. SECURITY

We apply reasonable security measures, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit
  • Access controls
  • System monitoring
6. DATA RETENTION

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the service
  • Comply with legal obligations

You may request the deletion of your data at any time.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request corrections
  • Request the deletion of your data
8. COOKIES

We may use cookies to:

  • Maintain login sessions
  • Improve the user experience
